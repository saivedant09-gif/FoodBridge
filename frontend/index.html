How FoodBridge Works

A streamlined, transparent food rescue workflow

1. Providers List Surplus

Restaurants, grocery stores, and caterers post excess edible food with shelf life, dietary tags, and pickup windows.

2. Recipients Request Food

Verified charities, food banks, and shelters claim matching items based on immediate community demand.

3. Reaches Those in Need

Secure pickup verification ensures prompt collection and distribution before food expiry.

For Food Providers

  • Instant Listing: Publish excess inventory with dietary labels, safe pickup windows, and quantities in seconds.
  • Verified Recipients: Only approved charitable organizations can claim food listings.
  • Impact Analytics: Real-time tracking of food saved, meals served, and emissions averted.

For Charity Recipients

  • Local Availability: Filter surplus food by proximity, food category, and expiration time.
  • Direct Allocation: Request exactly the quantity needed to serve your community.
  • Pickup Verification: Simple verification workflow ensures smooth and timely handoffs.
