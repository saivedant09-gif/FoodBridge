Environmental Value Added

When edible food decomposes in landfills, it releases methane—a potent greenhouse gas. By diverting edible food to charitable institutions:

  • Every 1 kg of rescued food prevents approx 2.5 kg of lifecycle carbon emissions.
  • Water embedded in agricultural production is preserved and utilized.
  • Non-profits reduce operational grocery budget and expand outreach.
